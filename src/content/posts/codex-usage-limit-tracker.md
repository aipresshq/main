## How the limit works

Codex usage limits govern access to agent-assisted coding work. The amount of capacity a task consumes can vary with its duration, repository size, selected model, and the amount of command execution or iteration involved.

## Where to check the reset

Look for usage and reset information in the Codex interface or the account area connected to the service. A notice shown during a session is the most relevant indication of what is limited and when access is expected to return. OpenAI's [official Codex account documentation](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11369540-using-codex-with-your-chatgpt-plan) should be treated as authoritative.

## Why usage varies

A small, focused change and a broad repository investigation do not place the same demands on an agent. Plan type, account eligibility, model selection, feature availability, and service conditions can also affect the capacity shown to different users.

Before beginning a larger task, check the account status and divide the work into reviewable stages. Keep commits or patches focused so progress remains usable if a limit interrupts the session. Avoid planning around cap figures from another account, because the product notice reflects the access actually available to you.
